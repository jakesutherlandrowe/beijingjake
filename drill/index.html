How it works

A module in three steps

  1. Go through every word in the module.
  2. Retry only the ones you got wrong, round after round, until none are left.
  3. Try the entire module in one go. Every word right with no pause and the module is complete.

What's a take?

Each run through the entire module is a take. Passing in one take is the dream.

Write them down

After each round write the words you missed by hand before you retry. You can also export them as a PDF.

Pausing

Your progress saves in this browser so you can stop any time. A paused take can't complete the module though. Finish it to see which words to retry.

Typing answers

Close answers count and small typos are fine. If the marking gets it wrong press I actually knew it. For English → Chinese type the characters or the pinyin with tone marks or numbers, like ni3 hao3.

Settings

Choose the test direction, how pinyin shows, simplified or traditional characters, the voice, and typing or marking yourself. Reset a module there too.

Keys

1 to 6 picks the pinyin. Enter checks and moves on. Y or N marks yourself.

No sound?

Check your volume and that your phone isn't on silent. The voices come from your device, so if it has no male Chinese voice you'll hear its default.

Keep it as an app

On a Mac in Safari choose File then Add to Dock. In Chrome click the install icon in the address bar. On an iPhone tap Share then Add to Home Screen.
